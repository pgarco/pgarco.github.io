The landscape of computational text analysis has fundamentally shifted. What was once a niche field requiring specialized technical expertise has evolved into a broadly accessible set of tools through large language models (LLMs). This democratization means that researchers from diverse disciplinary and methodological backgrounds now seek to integrate LLMs into their workflows systematically, moving beyond simple copy-paste interactions with web applications. As our communities of practice grow more methodologically diverse, we need shared vocabularies, pedagogical frameworks, and best practices that work across disciplinary and technical boundaries. 
This workshop brings together researchers and educators to address this critical juncture: How do we effectively talk about and teach LLM applications to audiences who lack programming experience but need rigorous, reproducible approaches to text analysis and beyond?
